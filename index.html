The important distinction

Connecting software gives AI access. Organizing knowledge gives AI context.

Google Drive, meeting transcripts, and calendar information can give ChatGPT useful reference material. But a connection alone does not tell AI what is approved, what is outdated, what is only an assumption, or what is still unknown.

In this webinar, Mark Perone will show how the Build Your Business Canon™ prompt evaluates that material and turns it into governed, reusable AI context.
